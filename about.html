About CalcHub

Last updated: October 2026

CalcHub is a set of free calculators for money decisions people make every month — mortgages, loans, credit cards, savings, paychecks and taxes — plus a few everyday tools. It is run by Sana, an independent publisher.

How we build the calculators

  • Every page shows the formula it uses and a worked example so results can be checked by hand.
  • Tax and limit figures come from official sources: IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 (2026 brackets and standard deduction), IRS Notice 2025-67 (2026 401(k) limits), the Social Security Administration (2026 wage base), HMRC, the Canada Revenue Agency and the Australian Taxation Office.
  • Rate references such as mortgage averages cite their source and date (for example Freddie Mac's weekly Primary Mortgage Market Survey).
  • Figures are reviewed when new tax years are published. The last full review was October 2026.

What our calculators are not

Results are estimates for planning. They are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice and do not replace a lender's Loan Estimate, a tax return, or a doctor. State and local taxes, credits and individual circumstances can change your real numbers.

Found an error?

Tell us on the contact page. We correct verified errors and note the date of the change.
