Body Mass Index for Adults

How BMI Is Calculated

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². In imperial units: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)².

Adult BMI categories (WHO / CDC)

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9Overweight
30.0 – 34.9Obesity class 1
35.0 – 39.9Obesity class 2
40 and aboveObesity class 3

Limits of BMI

  • Muscular people can score "overweight" with low body fat.
  • It does not show where fat is stored. Waist circumference above about 40 in (102 cm) for men or 35 in (88 cm) for women signals higher risk.
  • Some health bodies use lower cut-offs for people of South Asian, Chinese and other Asian backgrounds (e.g. 23 for increased risk).
  • Children and teens: BMI must be compared to age- and sex-specific percentiles. This calculator is for adults 20+.

This tool gives general information, not medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI? ▼
For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9.
Is BMI accurate? ▼
It is a quick population-level screening tool. It is less accurate for athletes, older adults, and pregnant women, and does not measure body fat directly.
What healthy weight range applies to my height? ▼
Enter your height above — the result shows the weight range that gives a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9.
