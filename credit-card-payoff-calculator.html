💳 Credit Card Payoff Calculator

Find out how long to pay off your credit card and how much interest you can save by paying more each month.

Credit Card Payoff Calculator

How Credit Card Payoff Is Calculated

Each month the card charges interest of balance × (APR ÷ 12). Your payment first covers that interest; only what is left reduces the balance. The calculator repeats this month by month until the balance reaches zero.

The three strategies

  • Minimum payment — modelled as 1% of the balance plus that month's interest, with a $25 floor. This is the formula most large US issuers use. Because the payment shrinks as the balance shrinks, payoff drags on for years.
  • Fixed payment — you pay the same dollar amount every month. Far faster, because the principal portion grows each month.
  • Pay off by a date — solves for the fixed payment that clears the balance in your chosen number of months.

Example: $5,000 at 24% APR

StrategyTime to pay offTotal interest
Minimum (1% + interest)19 yrs 6 mo$8,887
$150 / month4 yrs 8 mo$3,322
$200 / month3 yrs$2,001
$250 / month2 yrs 2 mo$1,449
💡 Raising a $200 payment to $250 on that card clears it 10 months sooner and saves about $550 in interest.

If you qualify, a 0% balance-transfer card (usually with a 3–5% transfer fee) or a lower-rate personal loan can cut the interest further. Compare the fee against the interest you would otherwise pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does paying more than the minimum hurt my credit score? ▼
No. Paying down the balance lowers your credit utilization, which usually helps your score.
How does minimum payment trap work? ▼
Minimum payment (usually 2%) barely covers interest. On $5,000 at 24% APR, minimums of 1% + interest take about 19.5 years and cost about $8,900 in interest.
Avalanche vs Snowball method? ▼
Avalanche: pay highest interest first (saves most money). Snowball: pay smallest balance first (psychological wins). Both beat minimum payments.
What is a good credit card APR? ▼
Excellent credit: 15-20%. Good credit: 20-24%. Fair credit: 24-29%. Rates vary by issuer — check your card agreement. Paying in full each month avoids interest entirely.
