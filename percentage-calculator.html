Percentage change vs percentage points

If an interest rate goes from 4% to 5%, it rose by 1 percentage point but by 25 percent. Mixing the two is one of the most common errors in news and finance.

Why a 50% drop needs a 100% gain

Percentage changes are measured from the starting value. $100 falling 50% is $50; getting back to $100 from $50 is a 100% rise. That asymmetry is why investment losses hurt more than equal gains help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount? ▼
Multiply the price by (1 − discount ÷ 100). A 30% discount on $60 is 60 × 0.7 = $42.
How do I reverse a percentage (find the original price before tax)? ▼
Divide by (1 + rate ÷ 100). A $108 total that includes 8% tax had a pre-tax price of 108 ÷ 1.08 = $100.
What is the difference between percent and percentile? ▼
A percent is a fraction of 100. A percentile is a rank: scoring in the 90th percentile means you scored higher than 90% of the group.
