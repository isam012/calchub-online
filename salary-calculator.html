How the Salary Converter Works

Everything is converted to an annual figure first, then divided back out. A standard full-time year is 8 hours × 5 days × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. Change hours per day or days per week and every result updates to match your real schedule.

Formulas

  • Hourly = Annual ÷ (hours per day × days per week × 52)
  • Weekly = Annual ÷ 52
  • Biweekly = Annual ÷ 26
  • Semi-monthly = Annual ÷ 24
  • Monthly = Annual ÷ 12

💡 Comparing an hourly contract to a salaried job? Contractors pay both halves of FICA (15.3%) and get no paid leave or benefits. A rough rule: the contract hourly rate needs to be 1.3–1.5× the salaried hourly rate to break even.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is biweekly the same as twice a month? ▼
No. Biweekly is every two weeks — 26 paychecks a year. Semi-monthly is twice a month — 24 paychecks. On $60,000, biweekly checks are $2,308 and semi-monthly checks are $2,500.
How many working hours are in a year? ▼
2,080 for a 40-hour week (40 × 52). Subtract paid holidays and vacation to get hours actually worked — typically 1,800–1,950.
How to convert annual to hourly? ▼
Annual ÷ (hours/day × days/week × 52 weeks). Example: $60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85/hour.
What is biweekly pay? ▼
Paid every two weeks — 26 times per year. Most common US pay frequency.
How to compare job offers? ▼
Convert all offers to the same unit (annual or hourly) for fair comparison.
